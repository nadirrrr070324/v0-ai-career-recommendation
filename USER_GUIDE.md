# CareerIQ - User Guide

## Welcome to CareerIQ

CareerIQ is an advanced AI-powered career intelligence platform designed to help you discover your ideal career path through intelligent analysis, comprehensive research, and expert insights.

---

## Getting Started

### Accessing CareerIQ
1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to your CareerIQ deployment URL
3. You'll see a professional cosmic-themed interface

### Initial Screen
The app opens with the **Start** tab active, displaying the career discovery form ready for input.

---

## Step-by-Step User Journey

### Stage 1: Career Discovery Form (Start Tab)

#### 1. Select Your Education Level
Choose your highest level of education:
- High School
- Diploma
- Graduate
- Postgraduate

#### 2. Add Your Skills
Click on the skills input field and select skills you currently possess:
- Examples: Programming, Data Analysis, Communication, Leadership
- Add multiple skills to get more accurate recommendations
- Remove skills by clicking the X button

#### 3. Select Your Interests
Choose areas that interest you professionally:
- Examples: Technology, Design, Business, Healthcare
- Select 2-5 interests for best results
- Your interests help find aligned careers

#### 4. Choose Your Preferred Industry
Select the industry you prefer to work in:
- IT
- Design
- Marketing
- Finance
- Healthcare
- Government
- Any (for no preference)

#### 5. Select Your Personality Type
Choose the personality type that best describes you:
- Analytical - Data-driven, logical thinker
- Creative - Innovative, artistic, imaginative
- Leader - Charismatic, decision-maker, organized
- Technical - Problem-solver, hands-on, detail-oriented

#### 6. Submit Your Profile
Click the "Discover Careers" button to analyze your profile and generate recommendations.

### Stage 2: Results Dashboard (Results Tab)

#### Understanding Your Results
After submission, you're automatically taken to the Results tab where you see:

**Career Readiness Summary:**
- Top Match Score: Your best career match percentage
- Top Careers: Number of recommended careers
- Current Skills: Your skill count
- Avg Readiness: Average readiness across all recommendations

**Career Cards:**
Each card shows:
- Career rank (#1, #2, etc.)
- Match percentage (with color coding)
- Career name and description
- Progress bar showing match strength

**Expanding Cards:**
Click any career card to expand and see:
- Why we recommend it
- Missing skills you need
- Learning roadmap (Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced)
- Salary range
- Associated mentors

### Stage 3: Detailed Analysis Tabs

#### Compare Tab
**Compare Up to 3 Careers Side-by-Side**

1. Select careers from your recommendations
2. View detailed comparison table with:
   - Match percentage
   - Required skills
   - Salary ranges
   - Growth potential
   - Mentors available
   - Learning roadmap
3. Use this to make informed decisions between careers

#### Skills Tab
**Understand Your Skill Gaps**

1. See your current skills
2. View skills needed for each career
3. Get a 12-month improvement roadmap:
   - Beginner level (Months 1-4): Basic skills
   - Intermediate (Months 5-8): Growing expertise
   - Advanced (Months 9-12): Mastery level

#### Trends Tab
**Explore Job Market Data**

For each recommended career, see:
- **Growth Projection**: 5-year career growth %
- **Market Demand**: Current job market saturation level
- **Salary Growth**: Expected annual salary increase
- **Industry Insights**: Market trends and opportunities

#### Mentors Tab
**Connect with Industry Leaders**

1. Browse 25+ industry-leading mentors
2. Each mentor shows:
   - Professional background
   - Career specialties
   - Areas of expertise
   - Learning paths they can guide
3. Learn from successful professionals in your target career

#### Interview Tab
**Prepare for Job Interviews**

1. Get 80+ role-specific interview questions
2. Access STAR method training:
   - **S**ituation
   - **T**ask
   - **A**ction
   - **R**esult
3. Learn universal interview tips
4. Practice answering common questions

#### Salary Tab
**Master Salary Negotiation**

1. Learn strategic negotiation tactics
2. Understand benefits beyond salary:
   - Health insurance
   - Retirement plans
   - Stock options
   - Remote work flexibility
3. See market rate comparisons
4. Get tips for maximizing your offer

#### Export Tab
**Generate Reports**

Generate professional career reports:

**Download as PDF**
- Professional formatting
- All career analysis included
- Save to your computer

**Email Report**
- Share via email directly
- Ready-to-send format
- Personalized subject line

**Copy to Clipboard**
- Quick sharing option
- Share on social media
- Include in applications

#### Chat Tab
**Talk to AI Advisor**

1. Type questions about your career journey
2. Ask AI insights about recommendations
3. Get personalized advice
4. Explore career strategies
5. Use quick prompt suggestions for ideas

**Example Questions:**
- "How can I transition to data science?"
- "What skills should I prioritize first?"

#### Details Tab
**Deep Dive into Career**

Click "View Details" on any career card to see:

1. **Career Overview**: Detailed description and opportunities
2. **Skills Breakdown**: Required skills with proficiency levels
3. **Market Data**: Growth, demand, and salary information

## Understanding the Match Score

### What the Percentage Means
- **90-100%**: Excellent match - highly recommended
- **75-89%**: Great match - strong fit for you
- **60-74%**: Good match - has potential for you
- **40-59%**: Moderate match - explore if interested
- **Below 40%**: Lower match - consider other options

### What Affects Your Match Score
- Education level compatibility
- Skills alignment (40% weight)
- Interest alignment (20% weight)
- Industry preference (10% weight)
- Personality type fit (10% weight)

## Color Coding Guide

### Results Display
- **Green**: High match (80%+) - Highly recommended
- **Blue**: Good match (60-79%) - Strong option
- **Yellow**: Moderate match (40-59%) - Consider exploring
- **Orange**: Lower match (Below 40%) - May not align

## FAQ

### Q: How accurate are the recommendations?
**A:** Our hybrid AI algorithm analyzes your education, skills, interests, industry preference, and personality type to provide highly accurate career recommendations. The accuracy improves as you provide more detailed information.

### Q: Can I change my answers after submission?
**A:** Yes! Click the "Start" tab to fill out the form again with updated information. Your new recommendations will generate instantly.

### Q: How long should I spend on each section?
**A:** 
- Career Discovery: 5-10 minutes
- Results Review: 10-15 minutes
- Full Analysis: 30-60 minutes
- AI Analysis: 10-15 minutes

### Q: Can I save my results?
**A:** You can export your report via the Export tab. Save the PDF to your computer for future reference.

### Q: What if I disagree with a recommendation?
**A:** All careers have valuable insights to explore. Even lower-ranked careers might surprise you. Check the "Why Recommended" section to understand the algorithm's reasoning.

### Q: How do I use the skill roadmap?
**A:** 
1. Identify missing skills from your recommended career
2. Follow the roadmap: Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced
3. Dedicate 3-4 months to each level
4. Use online courses, bootcamps, or certifications to learn
5. Track your progress and update your skills

### Q: Can I chat with real mentors?
**A:** The AI chatbot provides intelligent advice based on mentor profiles. For direct mentorship, you can research and reach out to mentors independently.

### Q: Is my data secure?
**A:** Yes. CareerIQ processes all data locally in your browser. No information is sent to external servers. Your privacy is fully protected.

## Troubleshooting

### Issue: Form not submitting
**Solution**: Ensure all fields are completed, then click "Discover Careers" button.

### Issue: Not seeing expected careers
**Solution**: Check your selected education level and skills. Try selecting different industries.

### Issue: Animations seem slow
**Solution**: Clear browser cache, refresh the page, or try a different browser.

### Issue: Mobile view looks distorted
**Solution**: Rotate device, adjust zoom level, or use landscape mode.
