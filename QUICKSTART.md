# Quick Start Guide - Celestial Career Hub

## 🚀 Get Started in 30 Seconds

### Step 1: Open the App
Visit the application in your browser (or click Preview)

### Step 2: Fill Your Profile
```
Education Level:  Select one (High School → Postgraduate)
Skills:           Type 3-5 skills (comma-separated)
                  Example: Python, Communication, Data Analysis
Interests:        Type 3-5 interests (comma-separated)
                  Example: Technology, Innovation, Problem-solving
Industry:         Select your preferred field
Personality:      Click one (Logical / Creative / Social)
```

### Step 3: Click "Discover My Careers"
The app calculates and shows your top 5 career matches!

### Step 4: Explore All 8 Tabs
Click through each tab to explore different features

## ✨ Tips & Tricks

### For Best Results
1. **Be Specific with Skills**
   - ❌ Don't: "computer"

   - Logical: Data-driven, analytical thinking

   - Select "Any" if you're open to options

4. **Explore Comparisons**
   - Don't stop at top match
   - Compare top 3 to make best decision

5. **Track Your Skills**
   - Be honest about current proficiency
   - Use roadmap as learning guide

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I change my answers?**
A: Yes! Just go back to the "Start" tab and fill out the form again. Your new recommendations will update automatically.

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
A: The algorithm uses a hybrid system combining skill matching, interest alignment, education fit, and personality type. Accuracy improves with more honest input.

**Q: Can I save my profile?**
A: Currently profiles are not saved (future feature). Take a screenshot or note your top careers!

**Q: Are the salaries accurate?**
A: Salary ranges are approximate based on 2025 Indian market data. Actual salaries vary by company, location, and experience.

**Q: Can I learn about other careers?**
A: The app includes 10 major careers. Fill out the form with different skills/interests to see other matches.

**Q: How do I use the mentor information?**
A: Research each mentor online - read books, watch videos, follow on social media. Apply their principles to your career.

**Q: Is the AI Chatbot really AI?**
A: It's an intelligent response system based on your profile. It generates contextual answers to your questions.

**Q: What if I don't like my recommendations?**
A: Try adjusting your inputs - change skills, interests, or industry to explore different options!

## 📞 Help & Support

### Having Issues?

1. **Form won't submit**
   - Ensure all fields are filled
   - Check if skills/interests are entered (comma-separated)
   - Verify personality type is selected

2. **No recommendations showing**
   - Try refreshing the page
   - Fill out the form again
   - Check browser console for errors

3. **Design looks off**
   - Clear browser cache
   - Try different browser
   - Check screen resolution

4. **Chatbot not responding**
   - Wait 1 second (it simulates thinking)
   - Try different question format
   - Check internet connection
