# Features At A Glance - Celestial Career Hub

---

## FEATURE BREAKDOWN

### 1. AI Career Matching Engine
**What it does:** Analyzes your profile and recommends ideal careers
**Input:** Education, skills, interests, industry, personality
**Output:** Top 5 career recommendations with match percentages
**Example:** You have Python skills + love coding → Software Developer 95%

### 2. Professional Results Dashboard
**What it does:** Displays recommendations in a professional dashboard
**Shows:** Top match %, total careers, your skills, average readiness
**Interaction:** Click to expand each career for details
**Design:** Summary stats cards + expandable career cards

### 3. Career Details Deep Dive Modal
**What it does:** Complete analysis of a selected career
**Includes:** Overview, required skills, learning roadmap, mentors
**Interactive:** Tabs for different information types
**Features:** Save career button, professional presentation

### 4. Skill Proficiency Tracker
**What it does:** Shows skills you need to develop
**Calculates:** Gap between current skills and career requirements
**Roadmap:** 12-month learning progression
**Levels:** Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced

### 5. Job Market Trends & Analytics
**What it does:** Shows career market data
**Data:** Growth projections, demand levels, salary trends
**Timeline:** 5-year projections with percentages
**Use Case:** Understand career growth potential

### 6. Mentor Connections Network
**What it does:** Connects you with industry leaders
**Mentors:** 25+ successful professionals in different fields
**Info:** Their expertise, specialties, learning areas
**Access:** See mentors for each career option

### 7. Interview Preparation Guide
**What it does:** Prepares you for interviews
**Content:** 80+ role-specific interview questions
**Methods:** STAR method training, role-specific tips
**Access:** Different prep for each career type

### 8. AI Career Chatbot
**What it does:** Answers your career questions
**Intelligence:** Understands your profile and recommends context-aware answers
**Conversation:** Natural language Q&A
**Topics:** Skills, salary, interviews, career paths, motivation

### 9. Career Comparison Tool
**What it does:** Compare 2-3 careers side-by-side
**Compares:** Match %, salary, required skills, mentors, learning paths
**Use Case:** Decide between multiple career options
**Interaction:** Select careers to compare instantly

### 10. Salary Negotiation & Career Growth Guide
**What it does:** Professional salary guidance
**Teaches:** 5 negotiation strategies
**Lists:** Financial, work, growth, and wellness benefits
**Data:** Average salaries for each career

### 11. Export & Report Generation
**What it does:** Creates your career report
**Formats:** Text file, email, clipboard
**Includes:** Your profile, recommendations, next steps
**Use Case:** Share with mentors, keep for reference
